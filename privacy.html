Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

Right On Time does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. There is no account, no login, and no analytics or advertising SDK. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your commute profiles, schedules, saved places, calendar-derived commutes, alarm settings, and commute history are stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database (SwiftData). They are not transmitted to us — we operate no servers and cannot see this data.

Information used to provide features

  • Location: With your permission, used only to set the starting point of a commute and compute drive times. Used while you use the app, never tracked in the background, never stored off your device.
  • Calendar: With your permission, read on your device to find events with addresses you may need to drive to. Calendar contents never leave your iPhone. You can disable scanning anytime.
  • Traffic & maps: Route and traffic-time requests go to Apple Maps (MapKit), the same service the Maps app uses, and are not tied to your identity.
  • Weather: Forecasts come from Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for your destination, governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We do not receive any of the above — it is exchanged directly between your device and Apple's services.

Purchases

In-app purchases (Right On Time Pro) are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details — only anonymized, aggregate sales information.

Data deletion

You can delete all app data anytime in Settings → "Delete all my data." This permanently removes every profile, schedule, place, and history entry from your device.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
