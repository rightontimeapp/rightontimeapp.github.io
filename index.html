Common questions

Will the alarm still ring if a traffic check doesn't run?
Yes. A safe wake-up time is always scheduled in advance — traffic checks only ever move it earlier (or later, if you allow). The alarm never disappears.

Does it work in Silent Mode or Focus?
Yes — Right On Time uses the system alarm, which rings through Silent Mode and Focus.

How do I get the most reliable overnight checks?
Plug your phone in and open Nightstand Mode before bed. iOS allows the most background activity while charging.

Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy. Your commute never leaves your iPhone, and there's no account.
